Questions

Does Forge apply to jobs on Upwork or LinkedIn for me?

No. Forge only reads public boards that allow it (Remotive, We Work Remotely, RemoteOK). It never logs into Upwork or LinkedIn, so your accounts stay safe.

Will it send emails without asking me?

Never. Sending is a dry-run by default. Real mail needs your own SMTP settings and the --send flag, and we recommend --test-only to your own inbox first.

What if someone says STOP?

Mark it with queue_outreach.py --stop q-ID and Forge never contacts them again.

Who is behind Forge?

Raghav, at Proximus. We build AI automation for small businesses and made Forge to stop wasting our own hours on gig hunting.

Is my data stored anywhere?

Self-install runs fully on your machine. Leads, drafts and the queue are plain files in your folder.

I don't use GitHub — can I still use Forge?

Yes. Download the ZIP starter pack (no GitHub account) or book Guided / Done-for-you setup and we install it for you. GitHub stays available if you want it.
